Before and during your date

1

Keep chats on the app

Get to know someone here first. Moving to WhatsApp too quickly makes it harder to report them.

2

Video call first

A quick video call confirms they're the person in the photos before you meet.

3

Meet in public

Choose a busy café, mall or restaurant. Never meet at their home or yours on a first date.

4

Tell a friend

Share who you're meeting, where and when. Send your live location if you can.

5

Arrange your own transport

Get there and back on your own, so you can leave whenever you want.

6

Trust your gut

If something feels off, it's fine to leave. You don't owe anyone an explanation.

Watch out

Red flags of a romance scam

Scammers follow a script. If you see these, stop and report.

Asks for money or gift cards
Whatever the story — emergency, flight, school fees — never send money to someone you haven't met.
Always avoids video calls
Constant excuses about a broken camera or bad network are a classic sign.
Pushes "investment" opportunities
Crypto or forex tips from a match are almost always a scam.
Falls in love too fast
Intense declarations within days, then a sudden crisis.

Report a member

Reports are confidential. Our team reviews every one, and the person is never told who reported them.

In immediate danger? Call the emergency number 112.
